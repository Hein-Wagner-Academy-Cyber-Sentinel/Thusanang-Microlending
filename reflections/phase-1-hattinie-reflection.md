What did I do?

I was build lead on the project for Phase 1. I checked the tool chain verification, set up the environment on my pc and worked on the documentation for the project. I worked on the Business Profile, Charter and did research for the business and to gain knowledge on the subject of microlending.  

What did I find hard?

Getting used to git hub, git hub commands, the various Ubuntu commands, and trying to figure out what the instructions I must follow to the best of my ability. Understanding how to commit, to push and to properly indicate what I did in that session. 
Getting and implementing the various versions of text formats and tools.

What would I do differently?

I would start with getting to know the various tools and commands better that we were given. I would also double check the files and documentation before I commit. Putting my work in a text or document format and have it reviewed by my teammate.  

What do I understand now that I did not before?

How connecting and communicating with my team mate on a regular basis does improve our productivity and understanding of the project. I need to remind myself when to ask questions and when to ask for help.
