# Reflections

**Phase: All year**

One reflection per student, per phase — not one per pair. This is where your individual voice is heard.

## What belongs here

- `phase-1-studentA.md`, `phase-1-studentB.md`, and so on for each phase.
- In each: what you did, what you found hard, what you would do differently, and what you now understand that you did not before.

## Evidence rule

Everything in this folder must be readable text: source files, transcripts, reports, or plain-language descriptions. No screenshots.
