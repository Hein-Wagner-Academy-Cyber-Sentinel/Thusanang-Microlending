# Cost Modelling

**Phase: Phase 5**

The platform costed in rand, with no account and no spend, using the public pricing calculators.

## What belongs here

- `bill-of-materials.md` — the costed design, with the exchange rate and date stated.
- `scenarios.md` — three models: as-is, cost-optimised, and the persona at three times scale, with the cost driver identified in each.

## Evidence rule

Everything in this folder must be readable text: source files, transcripts, reports, or plain-language descriptions. No screenshots.
