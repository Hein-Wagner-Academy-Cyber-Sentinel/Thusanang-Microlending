# Threat Model

**Phase: Phases 1 to 4**

The threat model grows with your understanding. Keep every version; do not overwrite. Each version shows how your thinking developed, which the panel will ask about.

## What belongs here

- `threat-model-v0.md` — Phase 1: assets, actors, entry points, and the three most material risks.
- `threat-model-v1.md` — Phase 2: which architecture controls address which risk.
- `threat-model-v2.md` — Phase 3B: risks introduced or closed by the built platform.
- `threat-model-v3.md` — Phase 4: what the AI feature and the incident simulation exposed.

## Evidence rule

Everything in this folder must be readable text: source files, transcripts, reports, or plain-language descriptions. No screenshots.
