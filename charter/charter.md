# Project Charter: Thusanang Microlending

## 1. Project Purpose

This project authorises the analysis, design, security planning, and staged development of a secure digital lending platform for Thusanang Microlending. The project aims to establish a business-aligned foundation for future architectural decisions while supporting secure digital loan origination and the protection of sensitive customer information.

## 2. Scope

The project will analyse, design, secure, and document a code-defined platform that supports digital loan origination for Thusanang Microlending.

The scope includes:

- Digital loan origination processes
- Customer information handling
- Threat modelling and risk assessment
- Security architecture development
- Identity and access management considerations
- Infrastructure and platform design
- Resilience and recovery planning
- Documentation and supporting project evidence

## 3. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

- A defensible architecture for secure digital loan origination is developed.
- Architectural and security decisions remain traceable to identified business risks.
- The proposed solution supports business growth and distributed operations.
- Sensitive customer and lending information is appropriately considered throughout the design process.
- The proposed architecture can be deployed and evaluated in a non-production environment to validate key design assumptions.

## 4. Assumptions

- Thusanang Microlending intends to expand beyond its current operating footprint.
- Employees may require access to business systems from multiple locations.
- The platform will process sensitive personal, financial, and lending-related information.
- Loan origination processes will increasingly occur through digital platforms rather than physical paper records.
- The project is based on a simulated business persona used for academic purposes.

## 5. Constraints

- Solutions should remain practical for a cost-conscious organisation.
- The design should avoid unnecessary operational complexity.
- The project will not use real customer information.
- The project is limited to the scope defined by the Build It Anywhere scenario.

## 6. Out of Scope

The following items are outside the scope of this project:

- Production deployment of a live lending platform
- Processing of real customer or financial data
- Operation of real banking systems
- Legal or regulatory certification activities
- Physical security systems and facilities management
- Day-to-day business operations unrelated to digital loan origination
- Use of funded or production cloud accounts outside environments controlled or approved for the project

## 7. Working Protocol

The team will work independently on assigned tasks before sharing drafts for review and consolidation.

The Build Lead is responsible for preparing implementation-focused artefacts, committing changes, and opening pull requests where required.

The Assurance Lead is responsible for reviewing proposed changes, verifying compliance with project requirements, and confirming that documentation and evidence standards have been met before approval.

The team will conduct regular check-ins throughout each phase and before major submissions. Additional meetings may be scheduled when significant uncertainties or disagreements arise.

Work handover will occur through repository commits, pull requests, shared documentation, and direct discussion between team members.

Where disagreements occur, both team members will review the relevant project documentation, lecturer guidance, and available evidence before making a decision. Decisions should be based on project requirements rather than individual preference.

No significant change should be merged into the main branch until it has been reviewed by the Assurance Lead.
