## 1. Project Purpose

This project authorizes the analysis, design, security planning, and staged development of a secure digital lending platform for Thusanang Microlending. The project aims to establish a business-aligned foundation for future architectural decisions while supporting secure digital loan origination and the protection of sensitive customer information. 

## 2. Scope

The project will analyse, design, secure, and document a code-defined platform that supports digital loan origination for Thusanang Microlending. 

The scope includes: 


•	Digital loan origination processes 

•	Customer information handling 

•	Threat modelling and risk assessment 

•	Security architecture development 

•	Identity and access management considerations 

•	Infrastructure and platform design 

•	Resilience and recovery planning 

•	Documentation and supporting project evidence 



## 2. Success criteria

The project will be considered successful if: 


•	A defensible architecture for secure digital loan origination is developed. 

•	Architectural and security decisions remain traceable to identified business risks. 

•	The proposed solution supports business growth and distributed operations. 

•	Sensitive customer and lending information is appropriately considered throughout the design process. 

•	The proposed architecture can be deployed and evaluated in a non-production environment to validate key design assumptions. 



## 3. Assumptions

•	Thusanang Microlending intends to expand beyond its current operating footprint. 

•	Employees may require access to business systems from multiple locations. 

•	The platform will process sensitive personal, financial, and lending-related information. 

•	Loan origination processes will increasingly occur through digital platforms rather than physical paper records. 

•	The project is based on a simulated business persona used for academic purposes.

## 5. Constraints

•	Solutions must remain practical and cost-effective for a cost-conscious organization. 

•	The design must avoid unnecessary operational overhead and architectural complexity. 

•	The project will strictly not use real customer or financial data. 

•	Implementation and evaluation are bounded by the parameters defined in the Build It Anywhere scenario. 



## 6. Out of scope

The following items are strictly outside the scope of this project: 


•	Production deployment of a live lending platform 

•	Ingestion, processing, or storage of real customer or financial data 

•	Direct operation of or integration with real banking systems 

•	Formal legal audits or statutory regulatory certification activities 

•	Physical security systems, perimeter monitoring, and physical facilities management 

•	Day-to-day corporate business operations unrelated to digital loan origination
