## Success criteria
_How we will know the platform is good enough. The platform must not only provide a safe place for the data but must also stave off attacks against the achitexture._

## Assumptions
_What we are taking as given. We are asssuming that a lot of data will have to be entered and cataloged. Also assuming the zero trust model whice inclued the "assume breach" principle._

## Out of scope
_What we are deliberately not doing this year. We will not be focusing on already setteled infastructure or how to secure infastructure that is already inplace._
